Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Have this text reviewed by a legal professional before launch.

What we collect

This app has no accounts and no server. Your language, theme, name, phone number and address are stored only on your own device. They are shared only when you tap WhatsApp or Call.

Location

If you tap "Use my current location", your browser asks permission. Coordinates are saved on your device and added to your WhatsApp message. An approximate place name is looked up through OpenStreetMap Nominatim.

Third parties

WhatsApp (Meta), Google Fonts and OpenStreetMap process data under their own policies.

Your choices

Clear your browser data or empty the fields in More to remove saved details.
