Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Have this text reviewed by a legal professional before launch.

What we collect

Your language, theme, name, phone number and address are stored on your own device so we can pre-fill your WhatsApp message. There are no user accounts.

Usage and request data

To process requests, prevent abuse and improve the service, the app sends activity to our server: pages viewed, buttons tapped, services you view or request, searches, your IP address and basic device information (browser, device type, screen size, language, time zone). When you request a service, we also receive the name, phone number and address you saved in the app.

How we use it

This data is used only to respond to your requests, operate and secure the service, and measure demand. We do not sell it. Data is shared internally with our operations team and with the service provider assigned to your request.

Location

If you tap "Use my current location", your browser asks permission. Coordinates are saved on your device and added to your WhatsApp message. An approximate place name is looked up through OpenStreetMap Nominatim.

Third parties

WhatsApp (Meta), Google Fonts, OpenStreetMap and our messaging/hosting providers process data under their own policies.

Your choices

Clear your browser data or empty the fields in More to remove saved details. Contact us to request deletion of data we hold.
